PA-LAM-A2E: Facial Expression Enhancement for Interactive Avatars based on Prosody and Action Bases

PA-LAM-A2E adds brow and eyelid motion to LAM-A2E while preserving its mouth animation. Speech energy and pitch determine when each movement occurs and how strong it is. For each event, the method blends two action bases: Open raises the brows and opens the eyes; Focus lowers the brows and tightens the eyelids.

EXPERIMENT NOTES

The three ablation configurations

B

LAM-A2E

The original model converts speech into facial blendshape coefficients. Its brow, eyelid, and mouth movements provide the baseline.

P

P-LAM-A2E

Each prosodic event drives a fixed brow raise. Its strength curve sets when the brows rise, reach their peak, and return to rest.

PA

PA-LAM-A2E

The full method uses the same base animation and event times as P. It blends Open and Focus for each event and adjusts the amplitude for the target avatar. Only brow and eyelid channels receive the added motion.

Speech and rendering · Section IV-A

The quantitative evaluation used 50 Mandarin utterances synthesized with edge-tts: ten each in five speaking tones. The clips on this page represent calm (T1), excited (T2), questioning (T3), serious (T4), and lively (T5) speech.

All methods use the same audio, avatar, camera view, and renderer. Each video shows the face above and the corresponding brow and eyelid close-up below, synchronized across columns.

Human evaluation · Section IV-D

Ten viewers each rated 30 individual videos: five speech clips animated with six configurations (EchoAvatar, EmoTalk, NVIDIA Audio2Face-3D, LAM-A2E, P-LAM-A2E, and PA-LAM-A2E). They scored upper-face naturalness and fit to speech tone separately.
